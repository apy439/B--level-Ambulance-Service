Privacy Policy

B-Level Ambulance Service – Hyderabad, Telangana, India

1. Commitment to Patient Privacy

At B-Level Ambulance Service, we treat the confidentiality of our callers and patients with utmost gravity. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and protect emergency booking information and medical transport details.

2. Information We Collect

When an ambulance is requested through our telephone lines (8317505354 / 9032628464), WhatsApp, or our online booking form, we collect only the information necessary to dispatch the vehicle and protect the patient:

  • Caller and patient names
  • Contact phone numbers
  • Exact pickup address and destination hospital
  • Relevant physiological condition or medical equipment needed (e.g. oxygen requirements, ventilator status)

3. Use of Information

All submitted details are used solely for real-time dispatch, paramedic preparation, routing, invoice billing, and direct clinical handoff with hospital emergency rooms. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade caller contact details with third-party marketers or advertisers.
