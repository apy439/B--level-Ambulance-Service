Basic Life Support (BLS) Ambulance in Hyderabad

Safe, reliable, and prompt medical transportation for stable patients requiring oxygen support, monitoring, and assistance across Hyderabad.

What is a BLS Ambulance?

A Basic Life Support (BLS) Ambulance is designed for non-invasive emergency situations and routine transfers where the patient does not need advanced cardiac life support or invasive mechanical ventilation, but still requires vital medical assistance, supplemental oxygen, immobilization, and professional lifting.

Our B-Level BLS vehicles (including our dedicated Maruti Suzuki Eeco ambulances) are equipped with clean hospital stretchers, emergency oxygen supply, BP monitors, pulse oximeters, and first-aid kits. They are operated by trained drivers and EMT assistants who know Hyderabad traffic routes intimately.

When to choose BLS?

Post-operative discharge, mild respiratory conditions, fractures, scheduled hospital appointments, diagnostic test transport, or routine patient bed-to-bed transfers.
