Advanced Life Support (ALS) Ambulance in Hyderabad

Specialized high-dependency ambulance with advanced cardiac monitoring, emergency drugs support, defibrillator, and trained paramedics.

Comprehensive Critical Care on Wheels

An Advanced Life Support (ALS) Ambulance is mobilized for patients experiencing acute, life-threatening conditions such as heart attacks, severe trauma, stroke, respiratory collapse, or unstable multi-organ symptoms.

Our ALS units (such as our heavy Force Traveler emergency ambulances) feature multi-parameter monitors displaying ECG, SpO2, NIBP, and temperature in real time. Our certified paramedic staff is prepared to administer life-saving CPR, airway intubation, and emergency stabilization while coordinating directly with receiving hospital casualty teams across Hyderabad.
