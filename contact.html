FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

How fast will an ambulance arrive at my location?

Our average arrival time in major Hyderabad localities (Gachibowli, Ameerpet, Banjara Hills, LB Nagar, Madhapur, ECIL) is approximately 10 to 15 minutes, depending on the exact pickup point and live road conditions.

Can a family member travel inside the ambulance with the patient?

Yes. Our ambulance cabins have dedicated attendant seats, allowing 1 to 2 family members to accompany the patient alongside our EMT crew.

Is oxygen provided throughout the transit?

Yes. All our vehicles are fitted with calibrated medical oxygen cylinders, regulators, and nasal masks to ensure continuous oxygen flow without interruption.

Do you handle long-distance transfers outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We provide outstation and interstate patient transportation across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and neighboring states with dual-driver shifts and clinical monitoring.
